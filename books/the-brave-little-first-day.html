📖 The Story

Maya woke up early, before the sun.
Her tummy felt like butterflies were dancing inside.

"Today is the day," Mama said, smoothing Maya's hair.
"The day you start your new school."

Maya nodded, but the butterflies wouldn't stop.

She packed her backpack: a pencil, an eraser, and one small seashell from the beach.
"The seashell is for courage," Maya whispered.

At lunch, Maya sat alone. The butterflies turned into a heavy rock.

Then — a voice.
"Is that a seashell?"
A boy with glasses and a gap-tooth smile leaned closer. "I have one too! A green stone from my grandma's garden."

And just like that, the butterflies started flying together.
